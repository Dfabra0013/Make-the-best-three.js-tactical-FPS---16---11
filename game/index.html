The round

E plant or cut the charge · B buy · attackers set a charge at either reliquary; defenders hold both

Accuracy counts

Your cone opens when you move and closes when you stop and crouch. The first round of a burst goes where you point it. Aim, then fire — running and gunning loses the duel.

Money matters

You start with $800 and are paid every round. A loss pays more the more you lose in a row. Buy a rifle, a plate, and a throw.

Three lanes

The Long Pan in the west, the Evaporator hall through the middle, the Weigh House in the east. Pick a lane, take a corner, watch your back.
